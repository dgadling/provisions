# Ingredients
- Base
  - ½ cup (8 Tbsp, 1 stick) unsalted butter at room temp
  - 1+¾ cup / 370g brown sugar - light/dark/mix/whatever
- Wets
  - 1 Tbsp vanilla (bourbon vanilla bean paste is great)
  - 2 eggs at room temp
  - 1 tsp salt
  - 1+¼ tsp baking powder
- 2 cups (240g) all purpose flour
- Inclusions
  - 1 cup chocolate chips (½ semi-sweet + ½ 60% cacao is nice)
  - ¾ cup semi-sweet mini chocolate chips

# Method
## Make dough
1. Take out the butter and eggs the day before so they come up to room temp
2. Set the oven to 325°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper and set aside
3. In a stand mixer with paddle attachment, on medium speed:
    1. Mix butter and sugar for 1 minute, scrape down sides, mix 1 more minute. Will not cream, this is fine.
    2. Scrape down sides, add wets. Mix 1 minute, scrape down sides, mix 1 more minute.
4. Set the mixer to low speed and:
    1. Add the flour, mix until _just_ combined
    2. Add the inclusions, mix _just_ until they distribute evenly

## Baking loop
1. Each cookie is 2 tablesppons (2 * 1 Tbsp scoop is great)
2. Only scoop out **5** cookies onto the sheet, they spread ***a lot***
4. Bake for 10 minutes
5. Take the sheet out and smack it on the counter 3 times to make the cookies deflate, put it back in for another 5 minutes
    * If you have a sheet cooling, move the now-cool cookies to a wire rack during those 5 minutes
6. Once the 5 minute timer goes off, smack the sheet on the counter 3 more times and set the whole thing aside to cool
