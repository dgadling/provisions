# Kath Bars (aka Chinese Chews, aka Food of the Gods)

Chewy tasty date & walnut bars. 
Ref: [this reddit comment](https://www.reddit.com/r/Baking/comments/1q1jfqz/comment/nx7icjv/)
## Ingredients

- 1c flour
- 1c sugar
- 1/2c butter
- 2 eggs (room temp)
- 1t vanilla
- 1/2t fiori di sicilia (or orange extract)
- 1t baking powder
- 1/2t salt
- 4oz chopped dates
- 1c fairly finely chopped walnuts. Some being powdery is fine.

## Preparation

### Batter

1. Cream butter & sugar
2. Beat in eggs, vanilla, fiori di sicilia
3. Fold in flour
4. Mix in chopped dates & walnuts

### Cooking

1. Preheat oven to 325
2. Grease a 9x9 pan
3. Pour the mixture into the pan and level it out
4. Bake for 45min
